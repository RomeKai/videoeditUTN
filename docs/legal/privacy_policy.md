# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 18, 2026

OneCreator ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI video processing platform.

## 1. Information We Collect

### User-Provided Information
*   **Account Data:** Email address, name, and password provided during registration.
*   **Payment Information:** Billing details processed securely via our third-party payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.

### Content and Media Data
*   **Source Files:** Video and audio files you upload for processing.
*   **Metadata:** Information generated by our Service, including transcriptions and AI scoring results.

### Technical Data
*   **Log Files:** IP addresses, browser types, and usage patterns for security and performance monitoring.

## 2. AI Processing and Face Tracking Data
Our Service utilizes specialized AI models for video optimization:
*   **Face Tracking:** We process video frames to identify facial landmarks for the purpose of intelligent framing (auto-reframe to 9:16).
*   **Biometric Data Disclaimer:** This facial analysis data is used strictly for technical video editing purposes. We do not create permanent biometric profiles or use this data for individual identification or authentication.
*   **Transcription:** Audio data is processed to generate text for subtitles and semantic search.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
*   To provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
*   To process transactions and manage your Workspace Wallet.
*   To ensure compliance with our Terms of Service and prevent fraudulent or illegal activity.

## 4. Data Storage and Third-Party Sharing
*   **AWS S3:** Media assets are stored securely using Amazon Web Services.
*   **AI Providers:** We may utilize third-party AI services (e.g., OpenAI Whisper) to process audio for transcription. These providers receive only the data necessary to perform the specific task.
*   **Security:** We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data both in transit and at rest.

## 5. Data Retention
*   **Media Assets:** We retain your original and processed videos as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request the deletion of your media assets at any time.
*   **Account Data:** We retain account information for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

## 6. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to:
*   Access the personal data we hold about you.
*   Request the correction or deletion of your personal data.
*   Object to or restrict the processing of your data.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
